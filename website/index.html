01 /Who we are

Our mission & vision

Working alongside Ottawa’s care homes, we bring what only a community can give: a companion who shares their language, their faith and their traditions. Through the Adopt a Muslim Senior program, volunteers commit to ongoing, respectful visits that offer genuine companionship and the assurance that they are remembered. Because in our community, no Muslim elder walks this journey alone.

Everpeace Companionship is a bilingual not-for-profit organization, federally incorporated under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act (CNCA), founded in Ottawa and launched on May 29, 2026. We currently serve the Ottawa area; our federal incorporation allows us to grow to other provinces as our resources allow, in shā’ Allāh.

Our mission

To reduce the loneliness and social isolation of Muslim seniors by building a structured network of volunteers and families who share their faith, language, and values, fostering meaningful relationships that promote dignity, spiritual well-being, and intergenerational connection.

Our vision

A community where no Muslim senior faces loneliness, sustained by a network of volunteers and families who provide companionship rooted in dignity, spiritual connection, and social belonging.

02 /Our values

What guides everything we do

“We serve with dignity, compassion and respect; we build meaningful companionship; and we strive for Ihsān in every interaction.”

Dignity

كرامة Karamah

We honor every senior as a person of inherent dignity, worth, and value—never treating them as a burden or merely as someone in need.

Compassion

رحمة Rahmah

We serve with mercy, kindness, empathy, patience, and genuine care, following the spirit of Rahmah that is central to Islam.

Respect

احترام Ihtiram

We honor our elders, their life experiences, wisdom, cultures, choices, and contributions. Respect for elders is explicitly emphasized in Prophetic teachings.

Companionship

صحبة Suhbah

We believe every elder deserves to be known, visited and remembered. We build meaningful, lasting human connections based on friendship, listening, presence, and belonging.

Service with Ihsān

إحسان Ihsan

We strive to serve with excellence, sincerity, reliability, and integrity—doing what we do for the sake of Allah and to the best of our ability.

How can we walk this journey together?

For elders who rarely have visitors

Our visits are first for Muslim seniors who have no family or friends nearby, whose loved ones live in another province, another country or a distant city, or who rarely receive visitors. We match each senior with a trained, screened volunteer of the same gender who shares their faith, language and values, for ongoing visits — conversation, company in prayer, a cup of tea, or simply a caring presence. 07 /For seniors & families

A familiar face who shares your faith

Some of our elders have no family or friends close by. Their children may live in another province, another country or a distant city, and visitors are rare. Everpeace Companionship exists first for them: a trained companion who shares their faith, language and traditions, so that no Muslim elder walks this journey alone.

Who our visits are for, first

  • Muslim seniors with no family or close friends in the Ottawa area
  • Seniors whose family lives in another province, another country or a distant city
  • Seniors who rarely receive visitors

Do you know a Muslim elder who rarely has visitors? With their consent, you can register them using the form below.

What a visit can look like

  • Conversation and listening, in a familiar language
  • Company in prayer, dhikr or listening to the Qur’an
  • A cup of tea, a short walk, or looking through family photos
  • Marking Ramadan and Eid together

08 /For health & social care professionals

Faith-sensitive companionship your clients can trust

Your team cares for every resident’s health and comfort. Everpeace Companionship adds what only a community can offer: a companion who shares their language, culture and faith, visiting in keeping with your home’s policies. We work alongside care teams, never instead of them.

For social workers, case workers, discharge planners, and recreation and spiritual-care staff in long-term care and retirement homes.

How referral works

  1. 1 Refer Invite the Muslim senior to complete our registration form. If they need help, fill it in with them, side by side, so that their consent remains their own.
  2. 2 Connect Our coordinator contacts the senior or family to understand needs and preferences.
  3. 3 Match We match a trained, screened volunteer of the same gender.
  4. 4 Visit Visits begin, following your facility’s visitor policy.

Built for safe collaboration

  • Signed senior or family consent form
  • Level 3 Vulnerable Sector Check for every volunteer
  • Incident reporting process — urgent matters follow your facility’s policy
  • One coordinator as your point of contact

10 /For volunteers

Your presence, their peace.

From first hello to first visit. You don’t need to be Muslim to volunteer, only kind, reliable and respectful.

Because belonging never retires.

Apply to volunteer
  1. 01

    Apply

    Tell us what motivates you to volunteer with Muslim seniors, the personal values and qualities you would bring, and when you’re available.

  2. 02

    Get screened

    We give you an authorization letter for your Level 3 Vulnerable Sector Check and complete Everpeace Companionship’s own screening.

  3. 03

    Train

    Begin with an orientation, then keep learning with the training resources on our volunteer platform — from brain health to the legal side of volunteering.

  4. 04

    Get matched

    Your coordinator applies our layered matching strategy to pair you with a senior, always of the same gender.

  5. 05

    Visit

    You visit as an Everpeace Companionship guest, following each home’s visitor policy — with your coordinator one message away.

11 /Support our work

No Muslim elder walks this journey alone.

Your gift becomes someone’s visit. Your generosity funds the people, the training and the small kindnesses that make each visit possible.

Why give to Everpeace Companionship

Sadaqah that becomes presence

Honoring and caring for our elders is a deeply rooted Islamic value. Your sadaqah turns that value into visits, so every elder is known, visited and remembered.

Consistency made easy

A monthly gift, even a small one, lets us plan visits, training and Ramadan support with confidence, all year long.

Local, lasting impact

Your gift supports Muslim seniors and the volunteers who visit them, right here in Ottawa.

Amanah: transparent & accountable

Every donation is treated as an amanah (trust), handled with care, integrity and full accountability.

Where your gift goes

Four priorities guide how every dollar is used.

Joy for our seniors

  • Wellbeing & essential needs Support for seniors’ wellbeing and essential needs, from comfort items to practical necessities, offered with discretion and respect.
  • Small tokens of care Meaningful little gifts that seniors receive during visits from time to time, a reminder that they are remembered.
  • Community program events Gatherings that bring seniors, families and volunteers together.

Well-prepared volunteers

  • Coaching & training Stronger coaching and training, so every volunteer visits with confidence and care.
  • Travel across Ottawa Help with volunteers’ travel costs, so they can reach seniors wherever they live in the city.

Appreciation & Ramadan

  • Ramadan grocery gift cards As Ramadan approaches, grocery gift cards for volunteer families, a gesture of appreciation and community solidarity.
  • Volunteer retention Recognition and support that keep our volunteers committed for the long term.

A strong, lasting organization

  • A dedicated team Fair pay for the officers behind every visit: our Coordinator, Executive Director, and finance, operations and marketing officers.
  • Responsible operations The administrative, financial, technology, insurance and operational costs, together with safeguarding, training and other essential expenses, that allow us to deliver our mission responsibly.
